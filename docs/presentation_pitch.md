# Pitch Deck Storyline — GreenVision AI

**Slide 1: The Invisible Threat**
Visual: A dried-up desert landscape.
Hook: "Every year, Bharat loses thousands of hectares to desertification. But by the time we see it on a map, it's already too late."

**Slide 2: Meet GreenVision AI**
The first "Always-on" environmental intelligence platform for offroad Bharat.

**Slide 3: Precision Vision (Duality AI)**
Showcase pixel-wise segmentation. "We see what the human eye misses—drought risk in every pixel."

**Slide 4: Real-Time Heartbeat (Pathway)**
Demonstrate the live metrics updating. "Not a static dashboard. A live pulse of the earth."

**Slide 5: Talk to the Earth (RAG)**
The demo where the user asks the AI about the forest's health. "Ask, and the environment answers—using live, streamed data."

**Slide 6: Conclusion**
"GreenVision isn't just a tool; it's a protector. For a greener, more sustainable Bharat."
